networking, partnership, design-partner, channel, endorsement, funding, or acquisition

detection / BCI model-compliance validation (excluded). "Direct customer" = end-buyers whose day
job is to purchase a pre-release audit (excluded). This list targets the value chain *above* us:
organisations that (1) produce or aggregate neural/clinical data, (2) provide data

platforms, (b) medical-workflow/clinical AI, (c) hospitals/health systems, (d) neurotech/BCI,
(e) medical data governance/compliance.

## Detail

**1. Datavant** — SF (Bay). *National health-data connection + de-identification; Privacy Hub does
HIPAA de-id and Expert Determination.* Category (e). **Why:** already the trust standard for
"pre-release compliance," but their de-id/Expert Determination is built for structured/clinical
data — **neural/EEG re-identification risk is a blind spot in their methodology.** NerveML can be
the neural-modality complement or an upstream tool for their determinations, reaching hundreds of
orgs already buying privacy services. Potential channel + endorsement + acquirer. *Entry:* public
stance "$40M+/yr on security & compliance infra"; approach via Privacy Hub / Expert Determination
team on the "neural modality gap." (execs 待核实) — datavant.com

**2. Verily Life Sciences** — South SF (Bay). *Alphabet precision-health platform; Pre/Workbench
provide AI-ready dataset sharing and a trusted research environment (TRE).* Category (a)/(e).
**Why:** Pre explicitly markets "AI governance, auditability, and security" and hosts sensitive
health data. NerveML's leakage-safe validation is the quality/compliance layer for neural/physio
datasets on that platform — embedding into an Alphabet-grade exchange is very high endorsement
value with pharma/payer/provider co-customers. *Entry:* their "built with a focus on AI
governance, auditability, and security" language; approach the platform-partnership team. —
verily.com

**3. Innovaccer** — SF (Bay). *Agentic AI healthcare cloud unifying clinical/operational/financial
data across health systems, payers, gov, life sciences.* Category (a). **Why:** aggregates large
patient datasets and ships AI agents, so it needs pre-training/pre-sharing compliance auditing. A
channel partner: once physiological/neural modules land on their platform, NerveML can be the
embedded validation layer reaching their hundreds of health-system customers; raised ~$275M
(early 2025, public reports) to build the "AI healthcare cloud." *Entry:* population-health /
responsible-AI product line. (people 待核实) — innovaccer.com

**4. NeuroPace** — Mountain View (Bay). *Implantable responsive neurostimulation (RNS) for
epilepsy; holds the world's only long-term intracranial EEG dataset.* Category (d). **Why:** a
uniquely valuable, uniquely sensitive neural-data asset; public company (NASDAQ: NPCE) already
using it to improve algorithms and open to research collaboration. If that data is ever shared or
used to train, it is the perfect NerveML use case — a **flagship co-customer / endorsement**, and
local. *Entry:* narrative "leverages the world's only long-term intracranial EEG dataset";
approach data-science / clinical-research collaboration. — neuropace.com

**5. Science Corp** — Alameda (Bay). *Clinical-stage, vertically integrated neurotech (retinal
interface + BCI) founded by ex-Neuralink co-founder Max Hodak.* Category (d). **Why:** the most
active post-Neuralink node in Bay Area neurotech; a clinical-stage company already facing the same
privacy/validation questions we solve. Value is **network + ecosystem signal** — reaching Science
means reaching the post-Neuralink talent and investor web. *Entry:* founder **Max Hodak** (public,
ex-Neuralink president). — science.xyz

**6. Emotiv** — SF (Bay). *Consumer/research non-invasive wearable EEG headsets (Insight, EPOC) +
developer platform.* Category (d). **Why:** one of the largest consumer-neurodata intakes, right
as 2026 neural-data privacy regulation tightens (Canada's privacy commissioner now classes neural
data as highly sensitive). They carry both reputational risk and a compliance-narrative need;
NerveML can be their "responsible neurodata" endorsement tool or a validation SDK on the dev
platform. CEO **Tan Le** publicly champions neural-data rights — aligned. *Entry:* responsible-
neurotech / developer-platform angle. — emotiv.com

**7. Cognixion** — Santa Barbara, CA. *Non-invasive BCI + AR assistive communication (Axon-R);
clinical trials pairing Apple Vision Pro with an in-house EEG headband.* Category (d). **Why:**
brands itself on "responsibly accessing neural signals non-invasively"; a natural ally that sells
responsibility. Clinical-stage EEG data needs exactly our privacy+validation — an ideal **early
design partner / joint case study**, and small (~29 people) means fast decisions. *Entry:* Axon-R
+ Apple Vision Pro trial as a news hook. — cognixion.com

**8. Paradromics** — Austin, TX. *Clinical-stage high-bandwidth implantable BCI (Connexus); did

standards co-builder. *Entry:* regulatory-affairs / clinical team. — paradromics.com

**9. Suki AI** — Redwood City (Bay). *Voice-driven clinical documentation assistant + Suki

(names 待核实) — suki.ai
